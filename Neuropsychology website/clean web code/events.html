Recent & Past Events

Connecting professionals and advancing neuropsychology

January 2026

International Health Symposium

Location: Mauritius

ANN participated in the first International Health Symposium in Mauritius, hosted by Curtin Mauritius and Charles Telfair Education. Our co-chair Dr. Ridwana Timol and her team organized an engaging and high-quality symposium.

Highlights: Global collaboration • Neuropsychology focus • Professional networking

November 2024

ANN Inaugural Meeting

Location: Hotel Sky, Sandton, Johannesburg, South Africa

The African Neuropsychology Network held its inaugural meeting, bringing together 14 core members from across sub-Saharan Africa. This historic gathering established the foundation for our collaborative network.

Highlights: Strategic planning • Member networking • Future direction setting
