Our Vision & Mission

Understanding our direction and commitment to advancing neuropsychology in Africa

Join the Network Explore Research

Our Vision

To use intellectual resources from within Africa to further our core mission of developing, networking, transforming, and mobilizing neuropsychology in clinical, research, and academic settings across Africa— by Africa, for Africa.

Our Mission

The African Neuropsychology Network is dedicated to advancing the field of neuropsychology across Africa through collaborative efforts among African neuropsychologists in academia, clinical practice, and research. Our mission encompasses four key pillars:

Developing

Advance neuropsychological knowledge, practice, and training programs that are grounded in African contexts and reflect the intellectual resources of the continent.

Networking

Foster meaningful collaboration and knowledge exchange among neuropsychologists, researchers, students, and institutions across Africa and globally.

Transforming

Reshape neuropsychology training programs and clinical practices to be culturally relevant, equitable, and inclusive of African perspectives and values.

Mobilizing

Activate resources, expertise, and collective action to address neuropsychological challenges and opportunities across the continent.

Core Principles

Guiding values that shape everything we do

African-Centered

Prioritising African intellectual resources, perspectives, and solutions to neuropsychological challenges.

Collaborative

Fostering partnerships and knowledge exchange across institutions, disciplines, and borders.

Inclusive

Welcoming diverse voices, experiences, and expertise from across the continent and beyond.

Evidence-Based

Grounding our work in rigorous research and best practices adapted to all African contexts.

Equitable

Ensuring fair access to resources, opportunities and representation for all members.

Sustainable

Building long-term capacity and institutional support for neuropsychology across Africa.
