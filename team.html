Growing Our Network

Our 14 core members are advanced-degree holders and leading professionals dedicated to advancing neuropsychology. Currently, all core members work at institutions in sub-Saharan Africa and beyond.

One of our primary aims is to ensure broader representation so that more African countries and educational institutions are involved in the network. We are actively seeking partnerships and welcoming new members who share our vision of advancing neuropsychology across Africa.
