Our Story

The African Neuropsychology Network was initiated on April 12, 2023, bringing together leading neuropsychology professionals across the continent. Our inaugural meeting on November 21, 2024, established the foundation for collaborative advancement of neuropsychology in Africa.

Funded by the INS Global Engagement Committee (GEC), ANN represents the first INS pilot regional network.

What We Do

Develop

Advance neuropsychological knowledge and practice through research, training, and innovation tailored to African contexts.

Network

Foster collaboration among neuropsychologists, researchers, and students across Africa and globally.

Transform

Reshape neuropsychology programs training and clinical practices to reflect African intellectual resources and values.

Mobilize

Activate resources and expertise to address neuropsychological challenges and opportunities across the continent.

Our Commitment

We are committed to ensuring broader representation so that more African countries and educational institutions are involved in our network. By leveraging intellectual resources from within Africa, we aim to create a neuropsychology that is relevant, equitable, and inclusive—reflecting the needs and strengths of African communities.
