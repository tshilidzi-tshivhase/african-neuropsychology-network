Our Mission

The African Neuropsychology Network (ANN) is an emerging network and the first INS GEC (pilot) regional network, led by a team of leading neuropsychology professionals in academia, based at various institutions across the continent.

Our vision is to use intellectual resources from within Africa to further our core mission of developing, networking, transforming, and mobilizing neuropsychology in clinical, research, and academic settings across Africa.
